## Training Modules Overview

| Module | Audience | Duration | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| Module 1: System Basics | All users | 30 min | Self-paced |

| Module 3: Stock & Procurement | Pharmacists, Procurement | 60 min | Guided + Practice |

---

## Module 1: System Basics (All Users)

### Learning Objectives
- Log in securely and use MFA.
- Navigate the dashboard.
- Understand your role and permissions.

### Key Topics
1. Logging in and MFA setup.
2. Dashboard widgets explained.
3. Notifications and alerts.
4. Logging out securely.
5. Password policy and how to reset password.

### Practice Activity
- Log in with your credentials.
- Find the "Low Stock Alerts" on the dashboard.
- Locate your user profile and note your role.

---

## Module 2: Dispensing Workflow (Pharmacists)

### Learning Objectives
- Dispense a drug against a valid prescription.
- Understand FEFO and batch selection.
- Handle Schedule X dispensing correctly.

### Key Topics
1. Dispensing flow: prescription → validation → batch selection → confirm.
2. FEFO — what it is and why it matters.
3. Barcode scanning during dispensing.
4. Dual authorization for Schedule X.
5. Viewing and printing dispensing receipts.

### Practice Activity (Sandbox Environment)
- Dispense 5 units of "Amoxicillin 500mg" against Prescription RX-DEMO-001.
- Attempt to dispense an expired batch — note the system block.
- Dispense a Schedule X drug with dual authorization.

---

## Module 3: Stock & Procurement (Pharmacists, Procurement)

### Learning Objectives
- Receive stock against a Purchase Order.
- Create and submit a Purchase Order.
- Perform a stock adjustment with proper documentation.

### Practice Activity
- Receive a demo GRN against PO-DEMO-001.
- Create a new PO for 100 units of Paracetamol from Demo Supplier.
- Adjust stock by -3 for BREAKAGE reason.

---

## Module 4: Controlled Substances (Pharmacists)

### Learning Objectives
- Understand Schedule X rules and legal requirements.
- Perform dual-authorized dispensing.
- Generate and verify the narcotic drug register.

### Key Message
> Mishandling controlled substances is a criminal offence. The system enforces dual authorization and maintains an immutable audit log of all transactions.

---

## Module 5: Admin & User Management (Admins)

### Learning Objectives
- Create, edit, and deactivate user accounts.
- Assign appropriate roles.
- Manage drug master and supplier records.

### Practice Activity
- Create a new pharmacist user account.
- Add a new drug to the drug master.
- Approve a new supplier.

## Assessment & Certification

- Each module includes a 10-question quiz.
- Passing score: 80%.
- Certificate issued on completion of all modules.
- Recertification: Annually or after major system updates.

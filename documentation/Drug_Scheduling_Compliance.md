# Drug Scheduling Compliance
**Pharmacy Management System**
*Version: 1.0 | Date: September 2026*

---

## 1. Overview

This document describes how the Pharmacy Management System enforces compliance with drug scheduling laws governing narcotics, controlled substances, and prescription-only medications.

---

## 2. Drug Schedule Categories (India — Drugs & Cosmetics Act)

| Schedule | Description | System Controls |
|---|---|---|
| Schedule H | Prescription-only drugs | Requires valid prescription scan |
| Schedule H1 | High-risk prescription drugs (e.g., antibiotics) | Dual-entry verification + prescription |
| Schedule X | Narcotic & psychotropic drugs | Dual authorization + physical log |
| Schedule G | Drugs taken under medical supervision | Warning label enforcement |
| OTC | Over-the-counter drugs | No prescription required |

---

## 3. System Enforcement Rules

### 3.1 Schedule H & H1
- Dispensing is **blocked** without a linked prescription ID.
- Prescription must be scanned or manually entered by a registered pharmacist.
- Monthly reporting to State Drug Authority is auto-generated by the system.

### 3.2 Schedule X (Narcotics / Controlled Substances)
- Requires **two pharmacist authorizations** before dispensing.
- Physical register (Narcotic Drug Register) must be maintained in parallel.
- System generates a daily reconciliation report (system qty vs. physical register).
- Any discrepancy triggers an immediate alert to the Pharmacy Manager.
- Supplier for Schedule X drugs must be an approved/licensed vendor in the system.

---

## 4. Quantity Limits

| Drug Class | Max Dispense per Transaction | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Schedule X | As per prescription, max 30-day supply | Requires DEA/NDPS license |
| Schedule H1 | As per prescription | Antibiotic stewardship alert if repeat |
| Schedule H | As per prescription | — |

---

## 5. Reporting Requirements

| Report | Frequency | Recipient |
|---|---|---|
| Narcotic drug register | Daily | Pharmacy Manager |
| Schedule H1 dispensing log | Monthly | State Drug Controller |
| Controlled substance reconciliation | Weekly | Compliance Officer |
| Drug disposal/destruction record | Per event | Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation |

---

## 6. Audit & Penalties

Non-compliance with drug scheduling rules is a criminal offence under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, 1985. The system is designed to prevent unauthorized transactions and generate evidence for regulatory inspections.
